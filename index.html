Privacy Policy

Gyanwani – Audio-Based Learning Platform

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Welcome to Gyanwani. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Gyanwani mobile application and its associated services.

Gyanwani is an audio-based learning application that allows users to explore educational audio lectures across different categories, subjects, topics, and instructors. The application provides free and premium audio content, subscription plans, individual audio purchases, and personalized learning features.

By accessing or using Gyanwani, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you use our application:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Name or display name provided during registration.
  • Mobile number used for account registration and OTP verification.
  • Email address, if provided.
  • Account login and authentication information.
  • Other information voluntarily provided by users.

1.2 Application Usage Information

To improve your learning experience, we may collect information related to how you interact with Gyanwani, including:

  • Audio lectures played by users.
  • Recently played audio content.
  • Listening history and playback progress.
  • Favorite lectures and saved content.
  • Categories and topics explored.
  • Subscription and purchased audio content details.
  • Interactions with featured and recommended content.

1.3 Technical Information

Depending on the application's configuration and enabled services, certain technical information may be collected, including:

  • Device type and operating system version.
  • Application version.
  • IP address and network-related information.
  • Application diagnostics and crash information.
  • Device identifiers, where applicable and permitted.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing secure login and OTP-based authentication.
  • Enabling access to audio lectures and learning materials.
  • Managing subscriptions and individual audio purchases.
  • Maintaining listening history and playback progress.
  • Managing favorite lectures and saved content.
  • Providing personalized recommendations.
  • Displaying featured and trending audio content.
  • Improving application performance and user experience.
  • Detecting unauthorized access, fraud, and security issues.
  • Providing customer support and responding to user queries.

3. Account Registration and Authentication

Gyanwani provides account registration and login facilities. Users may be required to provide their mobile number and other registration details to access the application's services.

OTP-based verification may be used to verify user identity and protect accounts from unauthorized access.

OTP information is used for authentication purposes and should not be shared with other individuals.

4. Audio Content and Listening Activity

Gyanwani provides educational audio lectures across multiple categories and topics. To provide application functionality, we may maintain information about your listening activity.

This may include:

  • Audio lectures you have played.
  • Your recently played content.
  • Playback progress and continue-listening information.
  • Your favorite lectures.
  • Your content preferences and interests.

This information helps us provide a personalized learning experience and allow users to continue listening from where they previously stopped.

5. Subscriptions and Purchases

Gyanwani offers free and premium educational audio content. Certain content may require a category-based subscription or an individual purchase.

When users purchase subscriptions or unlock individual audio content, relevant transaction information may be processed to provide access to the purchased services.

Payments made through Google Play Billing or other supported payment providers are subject to the respective provider's payment and privacy policies.

We do not intend to collect or store users' complete payment card details or banking passwords through the application. Payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment provider.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties. However, information may be shared with trusted service providers when necessary to operate and maintain the application.

These may include:

  • Authentication and OTP service providers.
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers.

  • Application performance and analytics providers, if enabled.
  • Technical support and maintenance service providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable laws, legal processes, or valid government requests.

However, no internet-based application or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.
